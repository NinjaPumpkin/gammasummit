## Section 3: 2. Not All Space Is Equal

Look at any chart.

You see price moving from one level to another.

What you don’t see — unless you understand exposure — is:

> The **quality of space between those levels**
> 

---

Two identical moves can behave completely differently:

- Move A: slow, choppy, reactive

[Positive gamma air pockets FINAL.mov](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-positive_gamma_air_pockets_final.mov)

- Move B: fast, aggressive, one-directional
    
    [Neg gamma air pocket.mov](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-neg_gamma_air_pocket.mov)
    

Completely different experience.

---

### The Difference?

> Liquidity density
> 

---

Where there is **high exposure (GEX)**:

- Dealers hedge actively
- Price encounters friction
- Movement slows

---

Where there is **low exposure**:

- Dealers have little to hedge
- Price encounters minimal resistance
- Movement accelerates

---

> The market is not a flat surface
> 
> 
> It is a landscape of friction and emptiness
> 

---

## Section 4: 3. Air Pockets — The Pathways of Price

### **Definition**

An **Air Pocket** is:

- A zone with **low or weak GEX exposure**
- Minimal structural resistance or support
- Limited dealer hedging activity

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-2.png)

---

### What This Creates

When price enters an air pocket:

- Movement becomes faster
- Reactions weaken
- Direction becomes cleaner

---

### Key Insight

> Air pockets are not targets
> 
> 
> They are pathways
> 

---

Traders make a critical mistake here.

### They see a move happening and assume:

> “That’s the trade”
> 

No.

The move is already **in progress**.

The opportunity existed **before price entered the air pocket**.

---

### Analogy

Think of nodes as walls and air pockets as open hallways.

- In a hallway → you can run
- In a crowded room → you slow down

---

> Price runs through hallways
> 
> 
> It grinds through crowds
> 

---

## Section 5: 4. Liquidity Vacuums — When the Pathway Expands

An air pocket can exist on a small scale.

A **Liquidity Vacuum** is the same concept — but larger and more dangerous.

---

### **Definition**

A Liquidity Vacuum is:

- A **wide region of weak or sparse exposure**
- Nodes are:
    - Thin
    - Weak
    - Widely spaced

Lack of floors or ceilings above

![Rug setup with a lack of floors. Price can go into free fall. *These are rare*.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-3.png)

Rug setup with a lack of floors. Price can go into free fall. *These are rare*.

---

### What This Creates

- Extended directional movement
- Minimal interruption
- Large, fast price travel

---

### Key Distinction

- Air Pocket → short pathway
- Liquidity Vacuum → extended runway

---

> If an air pocket is a hallway
> 
> 
> A liquidity vacuum is a freeway
> 

---

Once price enters a vacuum, it does not need to “fight” through structure.

It simply **delivers**.

---

## Section 6: 5. Rate of Change — The Missing Ingredient

Here’s where most traders fail.

They identify an air pocket…

But nothing happens.

---

### Why?

Because **space alone is not enough**.

---

### **Definition — Rate of Change**

Rate of Change refers to how quickly dealer positioning is shifting:

- Nodes increasing in magnitude
- Nodes decreasing in magnitude
- Nodes appearing
- Nodes disappearing

---

### What This Represents

> Dealer urgency
> 

---

If positioning is stable:

- Price can move slowly — even in an air pocket

If positioning is shifting rapidly:

- Price can accelerate aggressively

![Downside nodes of no particular interest at this moment. If we see rapid increase = bearish.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-4.png)

---

### The Relationship

- Air Pocket = **space**
- Rate of Change = **fuel**

---

> Space without fuel = drift
> 
> 
> Space with fuel = acceleration
>

## Section 7: 6. Velocity Mode — When Price Accelerates

### **Definition**

Velocity Mode is a powerful feature within Skylit that helps us identify nodes with high rates of change.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-6.png)

---

### The Result

- Greater sense of direction
- Minimal pullbacks if accumulation is aggressive
- Failed bounces
- Fast moves between nodes

---

> Velocity is not randomness
> 
> 
> It is positioning moving quickly
> 

---

### What It Feels Like

- “Price isn’t stopping anywhere”
- “Levels aren’t working”
- “Everything is getting run through”

That is **velocity**.

---

## Section 8: 7. Gamma Regimes and Velocity

Velocity behaves differently depending on gamma.

---

### **Positive Gamma Environment**

- Slower rate of change
- More controlled movement
- Stronger reactions at nodes

Air pockets may exist but can lead to a slow float up or down.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-7.png)

---

### **Negative Gamma Environment**

- Faster rate of change
- Increased dealer urgency
- Weakening reactions

Air pockets fulfill rapidly.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch8-image-8.png)

---

### Critical Insight

> Velocity Mode is most dangerous in negative gamma
> 

---

This is where traders get trapped:

- Trying to fade
- Expecting reactions
- Treating slow markets like fast ones
    - Theta burn

---

## Section 9: 9. Execution — What Traders Must Do

---

## **1. Do NOT Fade Velocity**

***If*** price is:

- In an air pocket
- AND accelerating

You are stepping in front of:

> Dealer flow
> 

---

***Fading here is not “contrarian”***

It is **undisciplined**

---

## **2. Position BEFORE Velocity**

The opportunity is not during the move.

It is before it begins.

---

Correct behavior:

- Identify weakening structure
- Spot rolling of ceilings or floors
- Anticipate expansion

---

Incorrect behavior:

- Chasing momentum
- Entering mid-move
- Reacting emotionally

---

> Professionals position early
> 
> 
> Amateurs chase late
> 

---

## **3. Respect Failed Reactions**

In velocity environments:

- Nodes tend to overshoot / break
- Reactions may be shallow due to strong momentum
- Structure may temporarily weaken

---

> Speed can override friction
> 

---

## Section 10: 11. Common Mistakes

---

### ❌ Fading Inside Air Pockets

You are trading against speed.

---

### ❌ Ignoring Rate of Change

You see space — but not the fuel.

---

### ❌ Treating Slow Markets Like Fast Markets

Different environments require different expectations.

---

### ❌ Confusing Velocity With Breakouts

Velocity is **not** a signal.

It is a condition.

---

### ❌ Over-Relying on Nodes During Expansion

Nodes lose effectiveness in high-speed environments.

---
