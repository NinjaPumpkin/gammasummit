## Section 4: 3. The Real-Time Thought Process

This is the exact sequence you should be running through.

Not perfectly.

But consistently.

* * *

## Step 1 — Start With Price

Before anything else:

-   What is price doing?
    
-   Are we trending?
    
-   Are we ranging?
    

![Trending down.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image.png)

Trending down.

* * *

> If price doesn’t make sense, nothing else matters

* * *

## Step 2 — Anchor to Structure

Now define:

-   Where are we relative to key levels?
    
-   Are we near support or resistance?
    

![Hitting major support level.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-1.png)

Hitting major support level.

* * *

You’re not guessing direction.

You’re asking:

> **Is there a location worth paying attention to?**

* * *

## Step 3 — Check the Map (Heatseeker)

Now you open the map.

You’re looking for:

-   The closest meaningful node
    
-   Strength of that node
    
-   Space around it
    

![SPY downside limited due to king node in proximity, QQQ bouncing off gatekeeper node at 593. SPX supporting upside.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-2.png)

SPY downside limited due to king node in proximity, QQQ bouncing off gatekeeper node at 593. SPX supporting upside.

* * *

Key question:

> **Does the map support what I’m seeing on the chart?**

* * *

## Step 4 — Evaluate the Node

Now slow down.

Not all nodes matter.

* * *

Ask:

-   Is this node strong?
    
-   Has it already been used?
    
-   Is it likely to react?
    

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-3.png)

-   If we compare this map to the chart, we can see that we were delivered from those upside nodes.
    
    -   They remain strong, we can expect a bounce because of the negative gamma nodes that can fuel a violent bounce (reverse rug setups).
        

* * *

> This is where most traders rush

* * *

## Step 5 — Expect the Reaction Type

Now decide:

-   Is this likely a direct tap of 593?
    
-   Or could this overshoot?
    

* * *

Reminder:

-   Most interactions = **direct tap**
    
-   Negative gamma nodes = higher chance of **overshoot first**
    

![QQQ overshot 593.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-4.png)

QQQ overshot 593.

* * *

> Prepare > Predict

* * *

## Step 6 — Check the Regime

Now zoom out slightly:

-   Are we sitting in a positive gamma pocket?
    
    -   Be quick to take profits. Positive gamma regimes lead to chop and theta burn.
        
-   Are we sitting in a negative gamma zone?
    
    -   Prepare for elevated volatility and aggressive price swings.
        

![SPX and QQQ can have volatile interactions. If we reclaim those gatekeepers, we can see an aggressive bounce because of those negative gamma nodes justly above spot.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-5.png)

SPX and QQQ can have volatile interactions. If we reclaim those gatekeepers, we can see an aggressive bounce because of those negative gamma nodes justly above spot.

* * *

You don’t need labels.

You just need to know:

> **Should I expect clean reactions or messy ones?**

* * *

## Step 7 — Check the Path

Now look at what’s around the level:

-   Are there air pockets?
    
-   Or is there gatekeepers / pika clouds?
    

> In the case study above, SPX and QQQ have clear air pockets straight to the upside. SPY may give us trouble through all that positive gamma.

* * *

This tells you:

-   How price will move after the reaction
    

* * *

## Step 8 — Confirm Across Indices

Now validate:

-   SPX
    
-   SPY
    
-   QQQ
    

* * *

Ask:

-   Are they doing the same thing?
    
-   Or is something off?
    

> SPX and QQQ are hitting collective floors in the case study above.

* * *

## Step 9 — Make the Decision

Now you decide:

* * *

### Everything lines up

-   Structure makes sense
    
-   Node is valid
    
-   Reaction is clear
    
-   Indices agree
    

→ **You can take the trade!**

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch11-image-6.png)

* * *

### If something is off

-   Weak node
    
-   Conflicting indices
    
-   Messy structure
    

→ **You wait or pass.**

* * *
