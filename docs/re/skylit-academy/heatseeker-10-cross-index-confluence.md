## Section 3: 2. The Core Principle — Markets Move as a System

Price movement is not isolated.

It is the result of **dealer positioning distributed across multiple indices**.

- SPX → institutional hedging
- SPY → liquidity + flow

These are not separate markets.

They are **three expressions of the same exposure engine**.

## Section 5: 4. Alignment vs Divergence

## Alignment (High Probability Environment)

Alignment occurs when:

- Nodes line up across indices
- Directional bias is consistent
- Structure confirms across all three

Like in the case mentioned above:

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch10-image-3.png)

---

### What Alignment Looks Like

- SPX at a king node with downside accumulation
- SPY with pika clouds preventing upside
- QQQ deflecting a king node+ pika clouds preventing upside

---

> 
> 
> 
> *This* is synchronized positioning.
> 

---

### Outcome

- Cleaner delivery
- Higher probability reactions
- Stronger follow-through
- Asymmetric risk to reward

---

## Divergence (Low Confidence Environment)

Divergence occurs when:

- One index holds, another breaks
- Nodes do not align
- Directional bias conflicts

---

### What Divergence Looks Like

- SPX holding support
- QQQ rejecting resistance
- SPY showing downside

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch10-image-4.png)

- SPX sitting on a king node floor
- SPY showing downside
- QQQ just below its king node

***SPX*** will prevent SPY and QQQ from breaking lower.

- SPX would need to break and hold below its king node for a flush down to SPYs king node.

---

> This is structural disagreement.
> 

---

### Outcome

- Chop
- Failed moves
- Fakeouts
- Whipsaw

---

> Divergence is not opportunity
> 
> 
> Divergence is a warning
>

## Step 5 — Classify the Environment

### Scenario A — Full Alignment

- All three indices agree
- Structure confirms

→ **High probability environment**

---

### Scenario B — Partial Alignment

- Two indices agree
- One lags or conflicts

→ **Reduced confidence but still playable - 2/3 confluence is bare minimum for trinity.**

- ***Our case study above falls under partial alignment. SPX has room higher.***
    - ***The idea is for SPY and SPX rug setups to play out, leading to a deflection to the downside.***

---

### Scenario C — Divergence

- Indices disagree
- Structure conflicts

→ **No trade or reduced size**
