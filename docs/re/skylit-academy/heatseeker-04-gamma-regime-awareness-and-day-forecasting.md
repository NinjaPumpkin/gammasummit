## **Why This Matters**

In Chapter 3, you learned how to read the map.

Now we answer a bigger question:

> _How is price likely to behave inside that map?_

Two traders can look at the same levels and trade completely differently.

-   The difference lies in someone knowing the gamma regime and playing appropriately to their environment.
    

When you pay attention to gamma regime:

-   You can anticipate slow markets
    
-   You can anticipate fast markets
    
-   You work with price instead of against it
    

Regime awareness doesn’t tell you direction.

It tells you **how price behaves based off dealer exposure on the maps**.

## Section 2: What is a Gamma Regime?

A gamma regime describes the **environment dealers are operating in**.

That environment changes:

- how fast price moves
- how strong levels behave
- how clean reactions are

There are two main regimes:

---

## **1: Positive Gamma Regime (Stabilizing Environment)**

This is a **controlled market**.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image.png)

![Choppy environment due to high density of positive gamma nodes.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-1.png)

Choppy environment due to high density of positive gamma nodes.

What you’ll notice:

- Price moves slower
- Rotations are tighter
- Levels hold more often
- Chop is common
- ranges are expected

Think of it like:

> **Price is being pinned within structure**
> 

---

## **Negative Gamma Regime (Volatile Environment)**

This is a **fast market**.

What you’ll notice:

- Price moves quickly
- Node overshoots happen more often
- Levels can bounce or reject fast
- Air pockets get filled aggressively

![Fast paced, aggressive candles with strong momentum high likelihood for overshoots.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-2.png)

Fast paced, aggressive candles with strong momentum high likelihood for overshoots.

Think of it like:

> **Price is free to violently move through the map**
> 

---

## **Key Rule**

Regime does **not** tell you where price goes… node accumulation and map reshuffles tell you where price is likely to go next.

Gamma Regime tells you: 

> **How price is LIKELY to move between levels**
> 
- Will it reject a ceiling and float down slowly? (positive gamma)
- Will it reject a ceiling and dump quickly? (negative gamma)

## Section 4: 3: Types of Days

Now we simplify this into something actionable.

Each regime tends to produce certain **types of days**.

---

## **Type 1 — Range / Choppy Day (Usually Positive Gamma)**

What it looks like:

- Price stays between a floor and ceiling
- Positive gamma predominates
- No confluence among the trinity maps
    - 1 index points up
    - 1 index points down
    - 1 index is pinned
- Price usually sitting on key level, anticipating news events

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-6.png)

How to think:

- Expect reactions at key levels
- Don’t chase reversals
- Respect both sides of the range
- Sell premiums (if experienced)
- Play the EXTREME ends of ranges ONLY

---

## **Type 2 — Trend Day (Usually Negative Gamma)**

What it looks like:

- Charts sitting on a key level
- Maps with nodes far from spot price, with rapid accumulation
- Velocity mode shows clear cut, one directional positioning
- Presence of air pockets
- ***Massive king node that grows rapidly with time***
- Floors / celings roll up / down
- Velocity mode (% change of nodes) sees rapid accumulation in one direction
    
    ![Velocity mode gives us real time insights as to where dealers are actively repositioning.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-7.png)
    
    Velocity mode gives us real time insights as to where dealers are actively repositioning.
    

How to think:

- Don’t fade strength blindly
- If missed entry point, sit out until clear pivot appears - like a king node price target getting hit.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-8.png)

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-9.png)

- Focus on structure in direction of move

## Rolling of ceilings/floors

- ***Rolling of ceilings*** - Considered strong presumptive evidence of a bearish thesis playing out.
    - Occurs when we see the upside ceiling and/or upside price targets decrease in value with the ceiling moving to a lower strike.
- ***Rolling of floors*** - Considered strong presumptive evidence of a bullish thesis playing out.
    - Occurs when we see the downside floor and/or downside price targets decrease in value with the floor moving to a higher strike.
- Understanding these behaviors of Market Makers can give us insight as to when we can see trends beginning to form and/or momentum in 1 direction.

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-10.png)

---

## **Type 3 — Whipsaw Day (Negative Gamma + Air Pockets)**

What it looks like:

- Fast moves through predominant negative gamma nodes
- Disorganization and chaos on the maps
- Lack of confluence among the trinity
- Violent reversals off extreme ends of ranges
- “Wicky” action

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch4-image-11.png)

How to think:

- Play extreme ends of ranges
- Wait for clarity to form
- When in doubt, sit out

Identifying Market Regimes gives us an important level of context as to what to expect during the day, giving us a clearer vision as to how to judge entries and exits.

- We can be correct on direction, but without proper execution, it will not matter.

## Section 7: Common Mistakes

### 1. Ignoring Regime

Trading every day the same way.

---

### 2. Forcing Breakouts in Positive Gamma

Expecting trends in a controlled market.

---

### 3. Fading Everything in Negative Gamma

Trying to catch reversals in fast conditions. Getting caught in drawdown during overshoots and force closing for loss while the trade reverses in your favor.

---

### 4. Skipping Charts

Jumping straight to Heatseeker.

---

## Section 8: Key Takeaways

- Regime defines **behavior**, not direction
- Positive gamma = slower, controlled markets
- Negative gamma = faster, expanding markets
- Types of days come from regime + structure
- Charts always come first
- Heatseeker confirms, not replaces
