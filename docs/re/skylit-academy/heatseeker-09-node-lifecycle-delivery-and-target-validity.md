Most traders treat levels like they are permanent.

- “Resistance is resistance”

## Section 4: 2. The Node Lifecycle

Every node goes through the same process:

### **1. Fresh Node**

- Untested
- Full strength
- Highest probability reaction

![AAPL node accumulation at All-time highs](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-image-1.png)

AAPL node accumulation at All-time highs

This is where you want to do business.

---

### **2. Tested Node**

- Price interacts
- Reaction occurs

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-image-2.png)

This is the first sign that liquidity has been used.

---

### **3. Delivered Node**

- Price rejects
- Movement occurs away from the node

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-image-3.png)

At this point:

> **The node has done its job**
> 

***The node can still pull and have influence, but with not the same amount of strength.***

---

### **4. Decaying Node**

- Influence weakens over time
- Becomes less relevant

[6700 was a major rejection point earlier in the day. Odds of reversion are slim. Calls off the table.](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-node_decay__ceiling_roll.mov)

6700 was a major rejection point earlier in the day. Odds of reversion are slim. Calls off the table.

### **Core Rule**

> **We do not target used levels**
> 
> 
> **We target fresh positioning**
>

## Section 5: 3. What Is a Delivered Node?

A delivered node is simple:

- Price tapped it
- It reacted
- Price left

That’s it.

But the implication is everything.

> **Once liquidity is used, it weakens**
> 

---

### Example Thought Process

You see a strong node at 600.

- Price taps → bounces
- Moves to 615

Most traders:

> “600 is strong, I’ll trade it again”
> 

Skylit logic:

> **600 has already been delivered from**
> 
> 
> It is no longer the highest probability level
>

## Section 7: 5. Real Nodes vs Hedge Nodes

This is where most traders get trapped.

---

### **Hedge Nodes (The Trap)**

- Often far OTM
- Large in size
- Look important
- But…

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-image-5.png)

> **They are protection — not intent**
> 

---

### Behavior:

- Do NOT grow
- Fade over time
- Lose relevance

---

### **Real Nodes (The Signal)**

- Can be near or far
- **Grow over time**
- Increase in magnitude

![image.png](https://d95fp8dfaxyli.cloudfront.net/academy/ch9-image-6.png)

---

### Behavior:

- Build
- Strengthen
- Act as real targets

---

### Core Distinction

> **Growth = intent**
> 
> 
> **Decay = protection**
>

## Section 8: 6. Targeting Logic — How Price Actually Moves

Retail mindset:

> “There’s a big node far away — price will go there”
> 

---

Skylit reality:

> **Price does not teleport**
> 

---

### Proper Logic:

- Far nodes = **possibility**
- Near nodes = **pathway**

---

### What actually happens:

1. Price moves to nearest structure
2. Reacts or builds
3. Continues step-by-step

---

> **Price is delivered through structure**
>

## Section 10: 8. Stairstepping patterns — How Trends Actually Form

## Section 11: 9. Common Mistakes

- Trading delivered nodes like they are fresh
- Confusing size with importance
- Chasing far OTM nodes
- Ignoring node growth vs decay
- Skipping structural steps
- Getting theta burned on distant targets

---

## Section 12: 10. Key Takeaways

- Nodes have a lifecycle
- Fresh nodes = highest probability
- Delivered nodes = reduced influence
- Price is delivered from node to node
- Growth matters more than size
- Structure dictates movement
- Stairstepping provides evidence of potential trend
